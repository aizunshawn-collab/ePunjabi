🎯 Step 2: Install Hindi Voice (Best Available Option)

📱 Windows 11 Hindi Voice Installation:

  1. Open Settings (Windows + I)
  2. Go to Time & Language → Language & Region
  3. Click "Add a language"
  4. Search for "Hindi" and select हिन्दी (भारत)
  5. Click "Next" and check "Text-to-speech"
  6. Click "Install" and wait for download
  7. Restart your browser after installation

Note: This will download ~100-200MB of voice data. Make sure you have a good internet connection.
